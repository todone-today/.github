# Code of conduct

todone is built in the open, and everyone taking part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives) is expected to:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions of every level.
- Keep feedback specific and useful.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or sexual content.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block people who break this. Report problems to **kushwah.kartikey@icloud.com**; reports are kept confidential.
