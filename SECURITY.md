# Security

Please report security problems privately to **kushwah.kartikey@icloud.com**, or through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" on the affected repo. Don't open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and what it affects. You'll get a reply within 7 days; please give us time to fix it before telling anyone else. We don't take legal action against good-faith research that respects people's data and stays within the [Acceptable Use Policy](https://todone.today/acceptable-use).

Supported: the hosted app at todone.today and the latest `main` of each repo.
